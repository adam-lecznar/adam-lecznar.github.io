Welcome to my site! I am a PhD student in Industrial and Entrepreneurial Engineering at Western Michigan University, specializing in ergonomics, occupational health and safety, and human-centered design. My work focuses on improving human-system interactions to enhance safety and performance, specifically in industries that lack standardization and rely on workers to overcome operational obstacles.

I am committed to lifelong learning and passionate about helping students discover and develop their potential. Outside of the classroom, I enjoy a wide range of physical activities, including hiking, strength training, and recreational sports. I also like to hone my technical skills by exploring new software tools.
